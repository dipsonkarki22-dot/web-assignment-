Nature is an amazing place that surrounds us. It consists of green trees, beautiful flowers, rivers with fresh water, and high mountains. The natural world provides us with clean air to breathe and water to drink. It gives us healthy food. Countless species of animals, birds, and insects live there. The beauty of the outside world is breathtaking all four seasons of the year. Fresh air is essential for our health and makes us think clearly. Being outdoors helps us feel comfortable and peaceful. It is like a picture made up of lots of small things that work together to keep everything alive on Earth. You can see nature in the sounds of a forest the way the ocean moves in and out and the tiny drops of dew on a blade of grass. Nature is not just pretty to look at with mountains and open fields it is also a system where every living thing plays a big part. Nature is a place where people can go to think and feel calm. It reminds us that we are a part of the world, around us. When we spend time in nature it helps us feel better. Makes us appreciate the world more. Nature is not something that is nice to look at it is a living thing that gives us what we need to live and we need to take care of it . Nature is what keeps us alive. It deserves our respect.
